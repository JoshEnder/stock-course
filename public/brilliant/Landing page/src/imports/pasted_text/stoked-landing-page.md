You are rebuilding the landing page for Stoked from scratch.

You have 3 visual references attached:
1. A Brilliant mobile landing/onboarding reference
2. A Duolingo mobile landing/onboarding reference
3. The official Stoked logo

Your job is to build a new landing page for Stoked that takes the RIGHT things from the references without copying them literally.

IMPORTANT:
This is not a desktop-first marketing website.
This is not a generic startup landing page.
This is not a mascot-led cartoon page.
This is not a cinematic hero experiment.
This is not a finance-bro dark dashboard.

This must feel like:
a premium, mobile-first learning product for beginner investors.

The page should communicate instantly:
Stoked is the easiest, cleanest, smartest way for beginners to understand stocks through short interactive lessons on their phone.

==================================================
CORE DESIGN DIRECTION
==================================================

Blend these ingredients:

FROM BRILLIANT:
- strong editorial typography
- intellectual confidence
- clean spacious layout
- restrained, premium product feel
- modern mobile-first composition
- product feels smart, not childish
- subtle visual systems / educational energy

FROM DUOLINGO:
- crystal-clear CTA hierarchy
- obvious next action
- approachable friendliness
- highly legible mobile structure
- beginner accessibility
- strong button design
- strong conversion focus

FROM STOKED BRAND:
- use the provided Stoked logo exactly
- preserve Stoked’s deep navy and green identity
- make Stoked feel more premium and ambitious than playful/childish
- beginner-friendly, but not juvenile
- investing education, but not intimidating

FINAL BLEND TARGET:
Brilliant’s intelligence + Duolingo’s clarity + Stoked’s finance-learning identity.

==================================================
PAGE GOAL
==================================================

The landing page has one primary job:
get users to join the waitlist / get early access.

Secondary job:
make Stoked feel credible, premium, simple, and worth trying.

This page must make a beginner think:
“finally, a clean way to understand stocks without feeling lost.”

==================================================
PLATFORM / RESPONSIVENESS
==================================================

Build mobile-first.
The mobile version is the priority.
Desktop should feel like an elegant scale-up of the mobile design, not a different site.

Design for:
- mobile first
- then tablet
- then desktop

On mobile:
- strong vertical rhythm
- large headline
- thumb-friendly CTA
- compact but breathable spacing
- no tiny text
- no overcrowding

On desktop:
- preserve the same hierarchy
- do not introduce giant empty dead zones
- avoid over-stretching the hero
- maintain visual tension and clean composition

==================================================
VISUAL STYLE
==================================================

Overall aesthetic:
- minimal
- editorial
- bright
- calm
- smart
- premium
- clean
- confidence without arrogance

Color direction:
- primary background: soft off-white, very light warm gray, or very pale green-tinted neutral
- primary text: deep navy
- accent: Stoked green
- borders: soft gray or desaturated green-gray
- avoid overusing gradients
- avoid loud multicolor sections
- avoid harsh blacks except maybe in tiny details

Typography:
- headline typography should feel bold, elegant, and high-confidence
- can have a slight editorial feel inspired by Brilliant
- body text should be clean, readable, modern, and quiet
- headline should feel premium and memorable
- no gimmicky rounded children’s font
- no generic startup font choices if they weaken the brand

Buttons:
- very strong CTA styling
- large rounded pill or rounded-rectangle buttons
- highly tappable
- clean shadow or depth if subtle
- primary CTA should feel irresistible and obvious
- secondary CTA should feel light and supportive

Cards / surfaces:
- use soft rounded corners
- subtle border emphasis
- minimal shadows
- clean spacing
- avoid clutter
- avoid “feature soup”

==================================================
BRAND EXPRESSION
==================================================

The Stoked logo should feel premium and central to the identity.

Do not distort the logo.
Do not redesign it.
Do not force extra mascot/cartoon language unless it is extremely restrained.

The product itself should be the hero.
UI previews matter more than decorative illustration.

If using supporting graphics, they must feel:
- clean
- refined
- finance-learning adjacent
- subtle
- not noisy
- not childish

==================================================
COPY TONE
==================================================

Tone:
- simple
- smart
- direct
- beginner-friendly
- calm confidence
- no hype-bro energy
- no cheesy motivational language
- no overexplaining
- no technical overload

Avoid words/phrases like:
- “revolutionary”
- “master the markets”

The tone should feel like:
“we made this clear, structured, and easy to follow.”

==================================================
LANDING PAGE STRUCTURE
==================================================

Build the page with these sections in this order:

1. HEADER
2. HERO
3. QUICK PROOF / TRUST STRIP
4. HOW IT WORKS
5. PRODUCT PREVIEW
6. WHY STOKED
7. FINAL CTA

==================================================
1. HEADER
==================================================

Header should be minimal and premium.

Include:
- Stoked logo on the left
- simple action on the right:
  - “Sign in” (secondary)
  - “Join waitlist” or “Get early access” (primary or more emphasized depending on layout)

Header should feel light and spacious.
Do not add unnecessary nav clutter.
No giant menu.
No fake enterprise nav structure.

==================================================
2. HERO
==================================================

This is the most important section.

Hero must communicate instantly:
what Stoked is,
who it is for,
why it feels different,
and what to do next.

MOBILE:
- logo/header on top
- headline
- short subhead
- CTA
- secondary CTA or trust line
- phone mockup or stacked mobile product preview

DESKTOP:
- two-column or smart asymmetric layout
- left: copy + CTA
- right: phone mockup / product preview

Hero headline options should feel in this territory:
- Learn stocks. Made simple.
- Understand stocks step by step.
- The easiest way to understand stocks.
- Learn stocks without the confusion.

Do not overcrowd the hero with too much copy.

Hero subhead should explain:
- short interactive lessons
- built for beginners
- made for your phone
- easy daily progress

Short interactive lessons that make stocks finally click. Built for beginners. Designed for your phone.

Primary CTA:
- Join the waitlist
or
- Get early access

Secondary CTA:
- Sign in
or
- See how it works

- use a PHONE mockup, not a laptop

- best screens to preview:
  - roadmap

  - lesson interaction

- no fake desktop app hero device
- no giant mascot as the main focal point

This hero should feel:

==================================================

Directly below the hero, include a compact proof section.

Use compact pills or refined cards, not bulky feature blocks.

Possible items:

- 5 min/day
- beginner-friendly
- free at launch

This section should reinforce credibility fast.

- 3 cards
or

2. Interact instead of just reading

- short heading

- optional minimal icon or tiny UI visual

This section should feel like product clarity, not marketing fluff.

Possible screens:

- roadmap

Important:

==================================================

End with a strong final section.

- one-line support text
- primary CTA
- optional email capture

Join the waitlist for early access.

This last section should feel clean and decisive.
